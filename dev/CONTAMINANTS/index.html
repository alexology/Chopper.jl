Contamination screen

chopper's -c contaminants.fasta removes reads that align to a contaminant (typically the lambda phage DNA control strand of ONT kits) with at least 90% of the read length as matching bases. chopper maps every read with minimap2 (Rust binding minimap2 0.1.31, minimap2 2.30) configured as Aligner::builder().preset(LrHq).with_cigar().with_index(fasta), and checks the "match length" (PAF column 10) of every reported alignment.

Chopper.jl does the mapping with Minimap2.jl, a native Julia port of minimap2 2.30, configured in the same way (src/contaminants.jl):

  1. Index (ContaminantIndex): MinimapIndex of the contaminant sequences with the lr:hq minimizers (k = 19, w = 19); the mapping options are then updated from the index, as with_index does (occurrence threshold from the 2·10⁻⁴ quantile, clamped to 50–500).
  2. Mapping: map_query with base-level alignment (F_CIGAR), which runs minimap2's seeding, chaining, chain selection and ksw2 alignment.
  3. Decision (Chopper): a read is a contaminant if the largest match length over its alignments is at least contaminant_fraction (0.9) times its length.

The index is only read while mapping, so the batches of chopper() share it across threads.

Until 2026-10-21 Chopper.jl had its own reimplementation of the same mapping (minimizers, chaining, banded alignment by anti-diagonals). Its match lengths differed from minimap2's for 0.4–61% of the aligned reads (co-optimal paths, narrower bands, Z-drop points), although the classification agreed on the validation data. It was replaced by Minimap2.jl so that there is one mapper in the suite.

Agreement with minimap2 and chopper

test/test_contaminants.jl compares the match lengths with minimap2 2.30 (-x lr:hq -c, largest per read; tools/make_contaminant_reference.jl) on chopper's test.fastq (250 reads up to 77 kb, ~10% errors) against random_contam.fa (one 75 kb read): identical for all 250 reads (182 aligned).

validation/compare_contaminants.jl, 20,000 ONT reads (ND2–COI amplicons, R10.4.1), minimap2 2.30 release binary (-x lr:hq -c --no-hash-name) and chopper 0.14.1 (-t 1 -c):

ContaminantsReads aligned (minimap2 / Julia)Match length identicalContaminants (chopper / Julia)Output identical
3 amplicon sequences of another sample15,761 / 15,76120,000 / 20,0000 / 0yes
32 amplicon sequences of the same and the other sample19,060 / 19,06020,000 / 20,00016,070 / 16,070yes

--no-hash-name matters: minimap2 breaks ties between equal chains with a hash seeded by the read name, and chopper, like Chopper.jl, maps reads without their names. Without the option one read (95b66141…, 2,833 bases, fraction 0.88 against two nearly tied references, below the threshold either way) has a different match length; it was first reported here as a Minimap2.jl discrepancy (2026-10-22: it is not one).

Speed (20,000 reads, chopper() with 4 threads): 24 s with the 32 amplicons and 24 s with the 3 of another species; chopper took 46 s and 63 s with one thread.
